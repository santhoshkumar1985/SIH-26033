## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution

KisanLink is a simple online marketplace where farmers and FPOs can sell produce directly to families, shops, restaurants and other bulk buyers. Farmers can list their crops, prices, quantities and harvest dates. Buyers can compare products and place orders. The platform also helps arrange pickup and delivery. It uses past orders and seasonal trends to estimate demand and plan delivery routes. This can reduce delays, waste and extra transport costs. By connecting farmers with more buyers and showing prices clearly, KisanLink can help farmers earn more while giving consumers a chance to buy fresh produce at lower prices. Its combined marketplace and delivery planning make it a useful, practical solution.

## Technical Approach

We will make the KisanLink app using Flutter and Python. Farmers can add produce, and buyers can place orders. The app will show prices and delivery routes. A simple AI tool will predict demand using past orders. For the prototype, we will show product listings, order placement and delivery tracking.

## Feasibility and Viability

KisanLink can start as a simple app in one area. Main challenges are internet access, delivery delays and different product quality. We can support local languages, help farmers use the app, check produce before delivery, and group nearby orders to save time and cost.

## Impact and Benefits

KisanLink can help farmers reach more buyers and earn better prices. Consumers may get fresh produce at lower prices. Grouped deliveries can reduce transport costs, food waste and fuel use. It can also help rural communities use digital services.
